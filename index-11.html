01 · The Problem

The Investment
Is There.
The Impact Isn't.

Title I district leaders are investing millions in High-Quality Instructional Materials, research-backed, standards-aligned curriculum designed to give every student access to rigorous instruction. Yet despite this investment, students are still falling behind.

District leaders are frustrated watching their curriculum investment stall at the classroom door, and the students who need the most continue to get the least.

40%
of students began the 2024–25 school year below grade level. National Center for Education Statistics, 2024, para. 1
52%
in high-poverty communities started the year below grade level. National Center for Education Statistics, 2024, para. 1
4.8
additional months of math instruction students now need just to recover. Sparks, 2024, para. 3

The problem Soaring Scholars LLC is solving is the gap between High-Quality Instructional Materials adoption and real classroom implementation for Title I district leaders, so that every dollar invested in curriculum actually reaches students.

Every child deserves a teacher who is fully equipped, deeply supported, and genuinely committed and every district deserves a partner who makes that possible.

02 · About

Janelle Jenkins

Founder & Lead Consultant · Soaring Scholars LLC

I'm an instructional facilitator, researcher, and consultant with 9+ years in K–12 mathematics education across Texas and Kentucky. My work lives at the intersection of high-quality instructional materials, instructional coaching, and turnaround school leadership.

As a Birdville ISD Instructional Facilitator and campus research-based curriculum point person for Bluebonnet Learning Math, I've coached 300+ teachers and partnered with TEA on the Strong Foundations Grant. I've seen firsthand what happens when curriculum investment stalls at the classroom door, and I built Soaring Scholars to fix it.

My doctoral research examines how professional virtue drives implementation fidelity in turnaround schools. Soaring Scholars LLC is the culmination of that work: equipping Title I districts with the tools, coaching, and expert partnership to ensure every dollar invested in curriculum actually reaches students.
