Meet Janelle Jenkins,
JJ — EdD Candidate

I'm an instructional facilitator, researcher, and consultant who spent 9+ years in K–12 mathematics education across Texas and Kentucky. My work lives at the intersection of high-quality instructional materials, instructional coaching, and turnaround school leadership.

As a Birdville ISD Instructional Facilitator and campus HQIM point person for Bluebonnet Learning Math, I've coached 300+ teachers and partnered with TEA on the Strong Foundations Grant. My doctoral research — "From Compliance to Commitment" — examines how professional virtue drives implementation fidelity in turnaround schools.

Soaring Scholars LLC is the culmination of that work: equipping districts with the tools, frameworks, and expert partnership to move from surface-level compliance to genuine commitment to excellence.
