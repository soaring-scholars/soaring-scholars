01

Instructional Workshop Day

Tier 1

A focused, on-site workshop experience for educators who need real support with high-quality instructional materials. We begin with the end in mind, work through internalization, analyze data, and build the practices that move students forward.

  • Curriculum internalization: understanding before delivering
  • Beginning with the end in mind — backward design in practice
  • Analyzing student data to inform instructional decisions
  • Building best practices for classroom implementation
  • Half-day and full-day options available

Systems Audit

By Inquiry

A deep-dive audit for campus and district leaders who need an honest look at what is working and what is not. We examine your PLCs, campus culture, and data systems to identify gaps and build a clear path forward.

  • PLC structure and effectiveness audit
  • Campus culture assessment and action planning
  • Data systems review — are you using data or just collecting it
  • Compliance to Commitment framework applied to your team
  • Findings report with prioritized next steps
