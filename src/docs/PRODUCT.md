# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents, teachers, and community members in Texas navigating the K-12 education system. Primary context: families who feel lost in the system, educators who lack support implementing high-quality instructional materials, and community members who care about local educational outcomes. Users come with urgency — a struggling child, a failing campus, a resource gap — and are looking for a guide they can trust.

## Product Purpose

Soaring Scholars is an education concierge: one trusted point of contact that connects parents, teachers, and community members to vetted experts, resources, and support — so no family has to navigate the education system alone. Success looks like a parent who found the right tutor, a teacher who finally understands their curriculum, a community that has a real partner in education.

## Brand Personality

Warm, authoritative, and community-driven. Voice: a trusted mentor and neighbor who happens to be the most knowledgeable person in the room. Not corporate, not preachy — direct, caring, and deeply competent.

## Anti-references

Big ed-tech / SaaS aesthetic: generic startup feel, gradient blobs, impersonal San Francisco design language, cream-and-muted-slate templates, "delight" animations that feel hollow. Soaring Scholars is a real person (Janelle) serving real families — not a platform.

## Design Principles

1. **Guide, not gate** — every page should reduce friction, not add steps. Information flows to the user, not through a form first.
2. **Authority through warmth** — credentialed and research-grounded, but never cold. The expert who explains things in plain language.
3. **The village is the product** — community, connection, and referral are the mechanism; design should make those feel real and accessible.
4. **Earn trust visibly** — show the founder's face, the credentials, the real testimonials. Trust is built through specificity, not stock-photo smiles.
5. **Every child deserves the best** — the mission is the design brief. Copy, hierarchy, and calls-to-action should always reflect this urgency without alarm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AAA. Audience includes community members and families who may use assistive technology. Reduced-motion support required. High-contrast text throughout. No time-limited interactions. Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements.
